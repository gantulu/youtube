# Image-to-Video Motion Planning

## Separation
Image-to-video instructions are separate from image-generation or image-to-image prompts.

## Motion specification
Describe supported motion for:
- subject/action;
- camera;
- environment;
- lighting;
- relevant audio behavior.

## Continuity
Motion begins from the generated scene state and must respect the analyzed scene start/end state. Avoid introducing motion that contradicts the source evidence unless explicitly requested as a creative transformation.
