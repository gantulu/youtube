# Final QA

## Required checks
- all intended scenes are present;
- timeline and ordering remain valid;
- character/object/environment continuity is preserved;
- visual prompts were executed against the correct references;
- animation and extension continuity are intact;
- audio remains synchronized;
- no blocking rendering or assembly defects remain;
- final output conforms to the validated project schema/requirements.

## Decision
PASS permits export. FAIL blocks export and routes the defect to the responsible production or upstream stage.
