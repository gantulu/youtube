# Production Record

Record the production result as an auditable handoff.

## Required record
- project/video identifier;
- production status;
- validation status;
- scene/asset references;
- final QA status;
- export artifact reference;
- relevant version information;
- blocking issues or warnings, if any.

## Principle
The record describes what was actually produced. It must not claim successful rendering, export, or verification without evidence.
