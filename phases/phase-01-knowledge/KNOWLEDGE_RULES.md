# Knowledge Governance Rules

## 1. Evidence
Do not convert an assumption or inference into a fact.

## 2. Authority
Prefer official documentation for product capabilities, parameters, limitations, and supported workflows.

## 3. Separation
Keep official facts, project rules, examples, and inferences explicitly separated.

## 4. Currency
Record source date/version where relevant. Outdated product behavior must not silently become a current rule.

## 5. Deduplication
Maintain one canonical statement for each rule. Link references rather than duplicating conflicting copies.

## 6. Traceability
Every canonical knowledge claim should be traceable to a source or explicitly marked as a project decision.

## 7. NotebookLM readiness
Documents should be self-contained, clearly titled, consistently structured, and free from unnecessary duplication.

## 8. Change control
Material knowledge changes must be recorded in the phase changelog and verified before becoming canonical.
