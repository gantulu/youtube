# Audio Analysis

Analyze audio in temporal relation to the visual timeline.

## Categories
- dialogue;
- narration;
- music;
- sound effects;
- meaningful pauses or transitions;
- speaker/voice continuity when observable.

## Timing
Associate relevant audio events with timestamps and the scene in which they occur.

## Evidence discipline
Do not invent dialogue, narration, lyrics, SFX, or speaker characteristics that are not supported by the available audio evidence.
