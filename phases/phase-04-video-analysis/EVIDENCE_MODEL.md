# Evidence Model

## Observed
Directly supported by the analyzed video evidence.

## Inferred
A reasoned interpretation that is not directly established. Keep it explicitly labeled and never present it as a product fact or observed event.

## Unknown
The available evidence is insufficient to establish the detail.

## Priority
Observed > Inferred > Unknown for descriptive confidence. When evidence conflicts, preserve the conflict and resolve it through additional analysis where possible.

## Anti-hallucination rule
Never fill an evidence gap merely to make a scene description complete.
