# Timeline Validation

## Checks
- complete source timeline is represented;
- scene boundaries are ordered;
- no unexplained gaps;
- no unintended overlaps;
- timestamps use the canonical project representation;
- scene start/end states align with adjacent scenes;
- rapid-event verification findings are preserved.

## Failure routing
Timeline defects route back to Phase 4 scene decomposition or Phase 5 scene generation, depending on whether the defect is in source interpretation or generation mapping.
