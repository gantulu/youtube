# Audio Validation

## Checks
- audio requirements map to analyzed audio evidence;
- dialogue/narration timing aligns with the scene timeline;
- music and SFX continuity is preserved where required;
- voice continuity claims remain evidence-grounded;
- unsupported audio content is not presented as observed.

## Failure
Route audio mismatches back to Phase 4 audio analysis or Phase 5 generation planning as appropriate.
