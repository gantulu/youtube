# Continuity Validation

## Identity
Verify persistent character and object references across scenes where applicable.

## Environment
Verify location/environment continuity and meaningful state changes.

## Action
Verify that the next scene can logically follow the previous scene's end state.

## Visual
Verify continuity of composition, camera language, lighting, and stable visual characteristics where the generation plan requires preservation.

## Failure
Route identity/reference defects to Global Reference or image-to-image planning. Route state/action defects to scene decomposition or generation planning.
