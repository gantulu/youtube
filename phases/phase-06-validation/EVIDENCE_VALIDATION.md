# Evidence Validation

## Objective
Ensure generated specifications remain grounded in the Phase 4 evidence package.

## Checks
- every material source-derived claim has evidence linkage;
- observed facts remain observed;
- inferred details remain explicitly classified;
- unknown details are not silently filled;
- generated transformations do not masquerade as source observations.

## Failure
An unsupported source claim is a blocking validation error unless explicitly framed as a requested creative transformation.
